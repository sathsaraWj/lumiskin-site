Camera and Photo Data

Lumiskin requests access to your device camera solely to capture face scan photos. By granting camera permission, you consent to the following:

  • Capture: The app captures a selfie photo when you initiate a skin scan.
  • Processing: Photos are cropped to your face area and compressed on-device before any upload.
  • Upload: Processed photos may be uploaded to secure cloud storage (Cloudinary) to enable AI analysis.
  • Analysis: The photo URL is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate visible cosmetic skin observations.
  • Storage: Scan photos may be stored for up to 90 days to support progress comparison features.
  • Deletion: You can delete your stored scan photos at any time from the app or by deleting your account.

We do not sell your photos, use them to train third-party AI models without your explicit consent, or use them for identity recognition, biometric profiling, or any purpose other than skincare analysis.
