Trust and privacy

Lumiskin provides cosmetic skincare guidance, not medical advice or diagnosis.

What Lumiskin is, and isn’t

Lumiskin describes visible, cosmetic features of your skin from a photo. It doesn’t replace a dermatologist or any other healthcare provider.

  • Observations describe appearance only, not medical conditions.
  • Lighting, camera quality, skin tone, and facial hair can affect results, which may be incomplete or incorrect.
  • If a spot or growth changes quickly, bleeds, or hurts, see a healthcare provider.

Careful with your photos

Your face photos are used for one purpose: generating your skin observations.

  • Photos are cropped, masked, and compressed on your phone before upload.
  • Photos are never sold and never used for identity recognition or advertising.
  • Guest scan results stay on your device.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the app.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Lumiskin do?

Lumiskin is a skincare app for Android. You take a guided selfie, and Lumiskin describes what’s visible on your skin, such as hydration, oiliness, breakouts, texture, and dark spots. It then suggests a morning and evening routine and helps you compare scans over time. It also includes an ingredient scanner for checking products.

Is Lumiskin medical advice?

No. Lumiskin provides cosmetic skincare guidance, not medical advice or diagnosis, and it doesn’t replace a dermatologist. Results are based on how your skin looks in a photo and can be affected by lighting, camera quality, skin tone, facial hair, and other factors.

If you have a skin concern, especially a spot or growth that changes quickly, bleeds, or hurts, see a qualified healthcare provider.

Is it available on Android and iPhone?

Lumiskin is available for Android on Google Play. iOS is coming soon. There’s no iPhone version available yet.

Can I try it before subscribing?

Yes. Lumiskin is free to download, and you can run a one-time guest scan without creating an account. Guest results stay on your device.

Eligible first-time subscribers may also be offered a free trial of Lumiskin Pro. If a trial is available to you, Google Play shows its length and the date your paid subscription starts before you confirm.

How are photos handled?

Your photos are used only to generate your skin observations. Before a photo leaves your phone, Lumiskin crops it to your face, applies a mask, and compresses it. It’s then processed by our cloud image-hosting and AI analysis providers.

Lumiskin doesn’t sell your photos or use them for identity recognition or advertising. Guest scan results stay on your device. For full details, including how long images are kept, read the Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel a Google Play subscription?
  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Select Lumiskin and tap Cancel subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period. Deleting your Lumiskin account doesn’t cancel your subscription, so cancel it in Google Play first. Refunds are handled by Google Play.
